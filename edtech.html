02 — EdTech & Training

Structured, instructor-led programs built for students, professionals and career-switchers who need real, job-ready skills.

AvenMinds runs structured, instructor-led training across IT, Non-IT and competitive exam preparation. Every program is built around outcomes — a job, a promotion, a passed exam — not just a certificate at the end.

Batches run in small cohorts with practical, project-based learning, mentor access, and placement support layered in from day one rather than bolted on at the end.

Why work with AvenMinds on EdTech & Training

1

Curriculum refreshed against current industry and exam requirements, not recycled slides

2

Small batch sizes so mentors actually know each learner's progress

3

Placement-linked tracks with real interview preparation, not just training

4

Flexible weekday, weekend and online formats for working professionals

5

Corporate and group pricing available for teams and institutions

How we work

01

Assess

We understand your current level and goal — job switch, upskilling, or exam clearance.

02

Build a Track

You're placed on the right IT, Non-IT or exam-prep track with a clear timeline.

03

Learn by Doing

Live sessions, projects and mock tests, not just recorded lectures.

04

Placement Support

Resume, interview prep and employer connects for placement-linked batches.
